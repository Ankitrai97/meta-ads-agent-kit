# The ad-writing skill

This is the playbook the agent loads when it writes ad copy, concepts and creative
briefs. `playbook/RESEARCH-AND-CREATIVE.md` covers the process; this covers the craft.

```
SKILL.md                         the workflow and the guardrails
references/ad-craft.md           hooks, copy frameworks, offers, creative briefs
references/campaign-settings.md  structure, budgets, Instant Form, handoff JSON
references/niche-playbooks.md    per-niche angles, offers, qualifiers, CPL ranges
```

The guardrails are the part worth keeping. Personal-attribute violations and
Special-Ad-Category mistakes are the two things that quietly destroy a local
campaign, and both are easy to write by accident.

The CPL figures in the playbooks are directional, drawn from US local service
campaigns. They are a sanity check, not a promise. Track cost per *booked job* in
your CRM rather than cost per lead in Ads Manager.
