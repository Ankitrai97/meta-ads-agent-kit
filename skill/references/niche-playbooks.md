# Niche Playbooks — angles, offers, triggers, qualifiers

Read the section for the client's niche. If the niche isn't listed, use the closest match + the universal rules at the bottom, and consider adding a new section as a learning.

---

## Roofing

- **Pains**: leaks after storms, insurance confusion, fear of being scammed, "how bad is it up there?" uncertainty.
- **Best angles**: storm/hail response (strongest — run within days of an event), insurance-claim navigation, free drone/photo inspection with adjuster-ready report, roof-age checkup.
- **Offers**: "No claim approved, no charge" risk reversal; named free inspection with deliverable; real insurance deadline ("60 days after the storm").
- **Seasonal**: spring/summer storms, pre-winter checks. Watch weather — a hailstorm is a launch trigger.
- **Qualifying Q**: "Is your roof over 10 years old?" or "Did your area get hit by the recent storm?"
- **CPL target**: $25–50 (single digits during storm windows).
- **Gotchas**: storm-chaser scam skepticism is high — lean on local proof, license #, real crew photos.

## Solar

- **Pains**: rising utility bills, distrust of pushy solar salesmen, "is my roof even suitable?", upfront-cost fear.
- **Best angles**: payment reframe ($0-down, "$X/mo less than your current bill"), bill-shock math, myth-bust ("solar isn't for rich people"), local rebate/incentive with expiry.
- **Offers**: savings estimate with a named deliverable ("custom bill-offset report"), rebate deadlines (real ones).
- **Qualifying Q**: "Do you own your home?" (non-negotiable — renters are junk leads) + monthly bill range if a second slot is ever justified — it usually isn't; pick the ownership question.
- **CPL target**: $40–80. Highest junk-lead risk of all niches — qualify hard, Higher Intent form always.
- **Gotchas**: "financing/approval" language → Credit special ad category risk. Say "flexible payment options". Educational tone outperforms hype — buyers have been burned by solar bros.

## HVAC

- **Pains**: uneven temps between rooms, surprise breakdowns in extreme weather, high energy bills, old system anxiety.
- **Best angles**: symptom hooks ("Is your bedroom 10° hotter than your living room?"), seasonal tune-up offers, payment reframe on replacements ("new AC for $89/mo"), heat-pump rebates ("up to $2,000 back").
- **Offers**: cheap diagnostic/tune-up ($49–$89) as foot-in-door; monthly-payment replacement framing; rebate deadlines.
- **Seasonal**: pre-summer (AC) and pre-winter (heat) are gold; run tune-up angles 4–6 weeks before the season turns.
- **Qualifying Q**: "Is your system more than 8 years old?" or "Own or rent?"
- **CPL target**: $35–55; emergency/repair angles run cheaper than replacement.

## Boiler / Heating

- **Pains**: no heat in winter (emergency), rising fuel bills, aging boiler dread, carbon-monoxide worry.
- **Best angles**: pre-winter safety check, emergency response speed ("heat back on today"), efficiency-upgrade math, payment reframe.
- **Offers**: named safety inspection; "same-day heat or the visit is free" style risk reversal (only if client can honor it).
- **Seasonal**: Sept–Nov is prime; mid-winter = emergency angles only.
- **Qualifying Q**: "Is your boiler over 10 years old?"
- **CPL target**: $30–55.

## Plumbing

- **Pains**: leaks/water damage fear, emergency availability, being overcharged.
- **Best angles**: emergency speed, flat/transparent pricing, water-heater replacement with payment reframe, camera-inspection named deliverable.
- **Qualifying Q**: "Is this an urgent issue or planned work?" (routes dialer priority — urgent leads get called first).
- **CPL target**: $35–55.

## Landscaping / Lawn / Hedge

- **Pains**: no time, embarrassment vs neighbors' yards, unreliable crews that no-show.
- **Best angles**: before/after transformations (best creative niche — use them), neighborhood-route offers ("we're on your street Thursdays"), seasonal cleanups, recurring-plan pitch.
- **Offers**: first-service discount into recurring plan; free yard assessment.
- **Seasonal**: spring cleanup, fall cleanup, pre-summer hedge/trim.
- **Qualifying Q**: "One-time cleanup or ongoing care?"
- **CPL target**: $15–35. Lower ticket — volume game; make sure the recurring upsell exists or economics break.

## Windows / Remodeling

- **Pains**: drafts/energy loss, decade-long procrastination, fear of huge quotes and pushy in-home salesmen.
- **Best angles**: payment reframe (essential — tickets are huge), energy-savings math, proof-story of a neighbor's project, "no-pressure exact quote" positioning.
- **Qualifying Q**: timeline ("Planning within 3 months / this year / just researching") — routes dialer follow-up cadence.
- **CPL target**: $40–70; sales cycle longer, so nurture automation matters more here than anywhere.

## Pest Control

- **Pains**: disgust/urgency, kids-and-pets safety, recurring infestations.
- **Best angles**: seasonal pest calendar (ants spring, mosquitoes summer, rodents fall), same-week service, safe-for-family framing.
- **Offers**: first-treatment discount into quarterly plan.
- **Qualifying Q**: "What pest are you dealing with?" (multiple choice — also gives the dialer context).
- **CPL target**: $15–35.

## Pools

- **Pains**: green pool embarrassment, unreliable maintenance guys, equipment failures in season.
- **Best angles**: before/after (green-to-clean), opening/closing seasonal packages, weekly-service reliability pitch.
- **Qualifying Q**: "Weekly service or one-time fix?"
- **CPL target**: $20–45.

## Universal rules (all niches)

- The buyer is a **homeowner**; ownership is the #1 qualifier when in doubt.
- Talk about the house/system/yard, never the person's finances or health (Meta personal-attributes policy).
- Local specificity lifts everything: city name in hook, county in storm ads, neighborhood in proof.
- The offer with a **real deadline** beats the same offer without one.
- Every package must set the "you'll get a call in under a minute" expectation — it lifts answer rates for the AI dialer.
