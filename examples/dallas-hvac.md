# Example conversation — fictional Dallas HVAC business

This is a teaching scenario, not a customer account or proven campaign. Do not copy its facts into a real user's run. No prices, claims, financing or results are assumed.

**User:** Help me set up my Facebook ads.

**Agent:** I'll help you plan the campaign, prepare the ads and connect the leads to follow-up. We'll review the setup before anything runs. What service do you want to advertise, and where do you serve customers?

**User:** AC replacement estimates in the Dallas area. I've never run ads.

**Agent:** Let's focus the campaign on replacement estimates. Which cities or ZIP codes will your team actually visit? Also, do you already have a Facebook business Page and ad account, or are you not sure?

**User:** I'm not sure about the account. Can you do that part?

**Agent:** Yes, I can help check it. Would you like me to use the browser tools available here to inspect your Meta business assets, or guide you through the screens? You'll enter your login and any verification details yourself. I'll check what exists before creating anything.

The agent inspects the actual supported UI/tools within permission, saves the result, and only then continues. If no browser tool is available, it says so and gives one next action. It doesn't report a connection from this example.

**Agent:** Before we create an ad account, here's the business owner, proposed account name, currency and timezone. Do these match what you want?

After the owner chooses the account details, the agent continues through offer/budget/capacity, research and the campaign plan. Missing details remain open questions, not invented “good defaults.” It creates a paused campaign only after the plan is approved.

**User:** I don't have photos ready.

**Agent:** We can prepare the copy now. Do you have any real technician or installation photos you can use, or would you prefer a concept image to review? If neither is ready, I'll give your editor a short brief and we can import the finished file later.

**User:** Let's stop for today.

**Agent:** I've saved the brief and plan in your private run. The creative is still pending, and nothing is live. Next time, say “continue my campaign” and we'll pick up with the images.

## What changes for another business?

The assistant asks for that business's service, actual geography, offer, budget and desired outcome. It doesn't assume Dallas, HVAC, “homeowners,” a static format or appointment booking. Existing campaigns/connections are inspected and reused within scope. A fresh account without results gets a measurement plan, not fabricated performance analysis.
