# Launch review

Preparation does not authorize activation.

- Intended account/Page and owner confirmed:
- Actual campaign/ad-set/ad IDs and paused statuses:
- Approved brief versus saved settings, evidence/date:
- Budget type, amount, currency, dates, timezone and maximum authorized total:
- Geography, destination, identity and applicable category:
- Copy/claims/assets approved and actual placement previews checked:
- Form/privacy/website and tracking verified:
- Test lead route, record/source and approved follow-up test evidence:
- Unresolved issues and limits:
- Current configuration digest from the local checker:
- Actual owner decision, scope, timestamp and source message reference:

If the setup changes, compare it again and refresh the relevant approval. Immediately before activation, re-read actual objects. After activation, record actual status, not an assumed performance result.
