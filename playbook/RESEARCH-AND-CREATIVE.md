# Research, copy and creative

Start with actual business inputs: reviews they own/can use, customer questions, offer, portfolio and previous campaign outcomes. Then research local competitors and public homeowner/customer questions. Save URLs, access dates, observed facts and campaign implications in `research.md`. If a claim is an inference, label it. Don't invent benchmark CPLs or call every opinion a market fact.

Use the Meta Ad Library via public UI or an approved data tool. Verify competitor service areas separately; finding an ad by keyword/country doesn't prove exact local targeting. Compare a few relevant examples by offer, first line, visual, CTA and destination. Visible dates can help prioritize what to inspect; 30-day-old ads do not establish continuous delivery, profit, conversion rate or booked jobs. Never copy competitor media, reviews or company-specific claims.

Use `ad-concepts.md` to propose distinct reasons to respond, rather than many superficial text variants. Three concepts are a useful small demonstration, not a universal test size. Fit the number to the actual budget and campaign. Each concept should include its audience question, offer/proof, primary text, headline, CTA, visual brief, destination and test hypothesis.

Write like the business speaks to its customers. Explain the service and next step plainly. Claims about savings, credentials, speed, financing, guarantees and reviews need owner-confirmed evidence. When evidence is missing, remove the claim or ask; don't pad the draft with pretend social proof. Advertising rules vary by business and placement: check the applicable current platform requirements for the actual offer rather than making a blanket compliance claim.

A static image is a practical first demo when the message fits; video may fit another service/offer. Prefer approved real business assets. For generated concepts, check factual/visual accuracy and never imply a fictional technician/project is genuine customer work. Give the user the option to import their own image/video if generation isn't useful. Don't let media render waiting become the main workflow.

Preview each actual placement/crop, including feed and vertical formats where used. Check legibility, safe areas, captions/video opening, identity, claims and message/destination consistency. Let the owner approve the concept, copy, assets and pairing. Then assemble only the approved ads paused through supported tools/UI. Read back the saved ads and previews. If a field/tool is unsupported, report it and show the manual route.
