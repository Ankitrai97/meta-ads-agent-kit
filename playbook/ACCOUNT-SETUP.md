# Account setup — existing account or starting from zero

Ask what exists, not for a list of account IDs the beginner may not recognize. Offer to inspect the official Meta interface together. Use [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/) and [Ads Manager](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/). Labels/eligibility can change: inspect the current screen and official help, don't blindly follow remembered coordinates.

## Nothing set up yet

1. Ask whether the user has their own legitimate Facebook login. Let them create/sign into that themselves. Never create a fake person or collect their password. They complete 2FA, identity checks and service agreements that need their judgment.
2. Check for an existing business Page before creating one. Ask for the exact business name, category, public business contact details and approved assets. Show the proposed Page before submitting it. Record ownership/access, not credentials.
3. Check for an existing business portfolio. Explain it as the place that groups business assets. Offer browser help to create one using the correct legal/business details if necessary; get the owner's decision on creating the actual resource.
4. In the intended portfolio, inspect ad accounts. Reuse an account when appropriate; otherwise offer to create one. Explain the proposed account name, owner/business, currency and timezone **before** creation; do not assume these are easy to change later. Record exact chosen values and approval.
5. Associate/assign the intended Page, Instagram identity if needed, and appropriate management permissions. Don't transfer ownership or grant a new partner broad access as a routine workaround. Request the missing asset access from the actual owner when needed.
6. Help the user find billing and readiness/status screens. The user enters payment information directly. Confirm readiness without copying financial details into files/chat. Investigate restrictions, required verification or creation limits through official Meta support/current help; don't bypass them.

## Existing account / “I'm not sure”

Inspect accessible assets first and ask the user to identify their business. Match the Page and ad account to the right owner. Verify name/ID, portfolio, currency, timezone, permissions, billing readiness and any visible restrictions. Mask identifiers in public screenshots. Reading an account does not establish permission to write or spend.

## What to save

In `connections.md`, record the intended account/Page identifiers, owner-confirmed identity, currency/timezone, permissions tested, readiness status, date and evidence location. Record missing access as a blocker. The `account_ready` check needs this evidence; do not pass it because the user merely said they have a Facebook account.

If Meta blocks account creation, say what the screen reports and give the user the next legitimate action. Work on the brief, research and draft ads while waiting. Account creation/verification cannot be guaranteed by this repository.
